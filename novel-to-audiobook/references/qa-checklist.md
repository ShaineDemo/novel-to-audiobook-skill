# QA checklist

## Source fidelity

- No plot fact, relationship, or chronology was changed.
- Narration and dialogue are attributed to the correct speaker.
- Names and uncommon terms have pronunciation notes.

## Visuals

- Recurring characters keep face, age, hair, clothing, and proportions.
- Locations preserve layout, direction, era, weather, and scale.
- Boats, buildings, tools, clothing, and crowds are physically plausible.
- Every image matches its associated text and uses the requested aspect ratio.

## Voices

- One stable Voice ID per identity.
- Adjacent scenes do not introduce unexplained rate or timbre jumps.
- Continuous letters and monologues were generated as grouped takes.
- Characters remain distinguishable without looking at the screen.

## Post-production

- Speech is always intelligible over SFX.
- No unnecessary continuous ambience.
- Cuts occur at natural pauses and do not clip consonants or breaths.
- Final loudness and true peak meet the project target.

## Timeline and delivery

- Start, end, and duration values match the final media.
- Text appears at or just after speech begins, according to the configured offset.
- All referenced files exist and use portable relative paths in manifests.
- Raw, grouped, cut, and final audio are kept separate.
- The delivery report lists assumptions and unresolved manual checks.
